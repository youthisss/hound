Baterai & Penyimpanan Energi

- Jenis baterai:
  • Lead-acid: 12V, murah, berat, cycle life ~500
  • Li-ion: 3.7V/cell, ringan, high energy density, butuh BMS
  • LiFePO4: 3.2V/cell, aman, cycle life >2000

- Kapasitas:
  • Satuan: Ah (Ampere-hour) atau Wh (Watt-hour)
  • Wh = V_nominal × Ah
    Contoh: 12V 100Ah = 1200 Wh

- Pengisian:
  • Lead-acid: constant current → constant voltage → float
  • Li-ion: constant current → constant voltage (no trickle!)

- Deep discharge:
  • Lead-acid: jangan <50% DoD (Depth of Discharge)
  • LiFePO4: bisa 80–90% DoD

- Untuk inverter:
  • Hitung arus DC: I_DC = P_AC / (V_DC × efisiensi)
    Contoh: 500W inverter, 12V, 90% → I = 500/(12×0.9) ≈ 46A

- Keselamatan Li-ion:
  • Jangan short circuit
  • Jangan overcharge/overdischarge
  • Gunakan BMS wajib!
